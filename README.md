# 🚀 CivicBridge

**Transparent Community-Government Feedback Platform**

CivicBridge is a modern feedback and issue tracking platform that connects residents with local government officials. Unlike traditional complaint systems where reports disappear without resolution, CivicBridge provides complete transparency — from reporting to resolution — and empowers communities to participate in governance.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

In many urban and rural areas, residents face frustrating red tape when reporting local infrastructure issues. Complaints often get ignored or lost, leading to distrust in governance systems. CivicBridge bridges this gap by offering a digital platform where issues can be:

✔ Reported with images and precise location  
✔ Tracked in real-time through multiple status stages  
✔ Upvoted and discussed by other residents  
✔ Managed and updated by government officials  
✔ Surveyed for feedback and public input

---

## 🎯 Key Features

### 🧾 1. **Issue Reporting with Tracking**
- Residents can report issues with photos and exact location.
- Each issue moves through the following status cycle:
  - **Submitted**
  - **Acknowledged**
  - **Assigned**
  - **In Progress**
  - **Resolved**
  - **Verified**
- Timestamps and responsible departments are logged.

### 💬 2. **Community Engagement**
- Residents can upvote and comment on issues.
- Trending issues dashboard reflects what matters most to the community.

### 🏛 3. **Official Communication**
- Government officials can respond with status updates, photos, and explanations.
- Publish surveys to gather structured feedback from citizens.

### 📊 4. **Analytics & Reporting**
- Track resolution rates, response times, and geographic patterns.
- Community journalists and advocates can analyze trends to identify systemic issues.

---

## 👥 Who Can Use It?

### 🧑‍💻 Residents
- Report infrastructure issues
- Track status of submitted reports
- Upvote and comment on community issues
- Participate in official polls and surveys

### 🏛 Government Officials
- Receive and categorize incoming reports
- Update issue statuses
- Publish surveys and gather public input

### 📰 Journalists & Advocates
- Monitor response times by department
- Identify trends and systemic issues
- Report insights to the public
